##The Momentum Anomaly
Stocks that have performed well over the past 3–12 months tend to keep performing well over the next 3–12 months, and stocks that have performed poorly tend to keep performing poorly — at least for a while. This directly contradicts strict EMH, which says past price performance shouldn't predict future returns at all.
